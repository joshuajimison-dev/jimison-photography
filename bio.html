Josh Jimison

Grew up in the North Carolina mountains. Studied chemical engineering, ended up in strategy, and takes pictures whenever he can get away from a spreadsheet.

Every season

Summers are for running and fall is for mountain biking. Once it gets cold, snowboarding becomes his entire personality.

Far from home

The weekend trips into the mountains kept getting longer. He's now been to 25 countries, all 50 states, and 40 of the 64 national parks.

Two cameras

Most of his work is shot on a Sony A7R III. The rest comes from an old Minolta film camera that was his dad's. Sometimes it's good to slow down and see what develops.
